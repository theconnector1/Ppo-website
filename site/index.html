How the Private Office works

How we begin, and how it runs.

For principal homes, second homes and estates, whether you live there all year or for a few weeks a season.

  1. A private introduction

    A conversation about the property, how you use it and what you would like taken off your hands.

  2. The property record

    We walk the house and grounds and build its record: a 3D record of the property, its systems, warranties, service history and the people who work on it. It belongs to you.

  3. Your limits, agreed once

    Below an agreed amount, we simply act. Above it, you receive one message with our advice and reply with a yes.

  4. Your property lead

    A named lead who knows the house, visiting as it needs rather than to a timetable, and in touch when something matters.

What you receive.

Short notes when something matters, and one statement of every cost. Both examples below are illustrative.

When we are most useful.

  • Before you arrive. The house checked, aired, at temperature and ready.
  • While you are away. Looked after in your absence, and checked after severe weather.
  • A new home. Represented at handover, with every defect followed up until it is put right.
  • Larger works. Advice from the brief, quotes checked before you commit, and the work overseen to completion.
  • Each year. A full review of the house against its record, and a plan for the year ahead.

The terms we keep.

  • No margin on the work. Supplier costs are passed on at cost. We are paid for our care of the house, never a share of what is spent on it.
  • Independent advice. We do not sell the work we recommend, and declare any connection first.
  • Discretion. Confidentiality agreed as standard.
  • Your record is yours. Every document stays with you, whatever happens.
  • One retainer. Set for your home, with the scope agreed in writing before we begin.
