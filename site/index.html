Questions.

How is the retainer set?
By the size and complexity of the home and how much attention it needs. We agree it with you after a private walk-through, in writing, before anything begins.
Do you add a margin to the work you arrange?
No. Suppliers are paid at cost and every receipt appears on your monthly statement. Larger projects are quoted separately and agreed with you first.
Who holds the keys?
Keys and codes are held securely and released only to people we have vetted. You will always know who has been in the house, when and why.
What happens if something goes wrong while I'm away?
Message or call us. We make the house safe, bring in the right people and keep you updated with photographs until it is resolved.
I already have a property manager or agent.
We work alongside them, on your side. Many owners keep their agent for lettings or building matters and leave the house itself to us.
Is this confidential?
Completely. Discretion is agreed in writing as standard. Your name, address and home never appear in our marketing.
