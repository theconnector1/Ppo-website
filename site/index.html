What is included

You pay for our care of the house. Never a margin on the work.

Small works are paid from your own upkeep fund at cost, with no mark-up. Our income comes only from the service itself.

  1. Onboarding

    An in-depth walk-through of the house and grounds, and a digital footprint of the property: aerial imagery, a 360° walkthrough and a full record of its rooms, systems and condition. It is yours to keep, and it is refreshed every year.

  2. Monthly retainer

    Our visits, reports, administration, your live statement and being on call. It includes arranging and overseeing every small job. Set for your home at onboarding.

  3. Upkeep fund

    An agreed amount you deposit, held in a separate client account. Small works are paid from it at cost, and trades invoice you directly. We let you know when it needs topping up.

  4. Projects

    Larger works above your limit, managed on your side from brief to handover, for a fee agreed before anything starts.

  5. Household services

    Preparing the house for your arrival, coordinating housekeepers, gardeners and pool staff, deliveries and access, key holding and seasonal changeovers.
