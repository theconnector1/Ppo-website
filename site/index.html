What we look after.

  • While you are away. The house checked, secured and looked after, and attended after severe weather.
  • Before you arrive. Aired, at temperature and ready, with anything outstanding resolved.
  • Keys and access. Held securely, with every visitor to the house arranged and attended.
  • Staff and suppliers. Housekeepers, gardeners, pool and system specialists coordinated.
  • Repairs and small works. Arranged, overseen and checked, by people we trust.
  • Deliveries and appointments. Received and handled at the house on your behalf.

How it works.

  • A monthly retainer. Covers our attendance at the house, being on call and your statement.
  • Each task agreed. Anything beyond the retainer is agreed with you first and shown on your statement.
  • Your limits, agreed once. Below an agreed amount, we simply act. Above it, one message and a yes from you.
  • One named contact. Someone who knows the house and answers when you call.
  • We settle the invoices. Supplier costs passed on at cost, with receipts, never marked up.

What you receive.

A short note when something matters, and one statement of every cost. Both examples below are illustrative.

How the Private Office begins.

For principal homes, second homes and estates. Most clients begin with the Concierge.

  1. The property record

    We walk the house and grounds and build its record: the property, its systems, warranties, service history and the people who work on it. It belongs to you.

    What it needs this year and beyond, in plain terms, with costs set out before anything is committed.

    A named lead who knows the house, visiting as it needs rather than to a timetable, and in touch when something matters.

  4. Each year

    A full review of the house against its record, and a plan for the year ahead.

The terms we keep.

  • No margin on the work. We are paid for our care of the house, never a share of what is spent on it.

  • Discretion. Confidentiality agreed as standard.
  • Your record is yours. Every document stays with you, whatever happens.
  • Agreed in writing. The scope and your limits are set out before we begin.
